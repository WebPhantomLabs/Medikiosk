# MediKiosk

## Problem Overview
Large government OPDs see very high patient volumes, often requiring doctors to consult 100–200 patients within a few hours. This typically leaves only 2–3 minutes per patient.

In that short window, doctors must:
- Review old handwritten prescriptions and lab reports.
- Capture detailed history and current symptoms.
- In Ayurvedic settings, assess lifestyle and constitution dimensions such as Agni, Koshtha, Prakriti, and Ahara-Vihara.
- Perform examination, diagnosis, and treatment planning.

This compression of clinical workflow increases the chance of missed details, clinician burnout, and rushed care.

## Solution
MediKiosk is a smart, voice-enabled digital intake terminal in the hospital waiting area. It acts as a virtual junior doctor before the consultation, so clinicians receive a structured summary in advance.

### Core Capabilities
- Multilingual patient interaction (Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, and other local languages).
- Voice symptom capture with dynamic follow-up questioning.
- AI-assisted scanning and digitization of old paper prescriptions and lab reports.
- Structured one-page clinical summary generation for doctors.
- Emergency symptom detection with rapid triage alerting.

## End-to-End 5-Step Workflow

### 1) Check-in and Verification
- Patient selects language.
- Patient authenticates via ABHA ID or mobile number.
- Kiosk provides audio privacy notice and captures spoken/one-tap consent.

### 2) Voice-Guided Triage Conversation
- Kiosk asks for the chief complaint in natural language.
- AI asks 3–4 adaptive follow-up questions (for example: duration, severity, digestion, bowel habits, and daily routine).
- Responses are captured in a structured intake format.

### 3) Document Scanning
- Patient places old prescriptions/reports under scanner.
- AI camera extracts key data from handwritten and printed material:
  - prior diagnoses
  - prior medications
  - relevant lab values and abnormalities

### 4) Clinical Summary Generation
- Voice intake + scanned document data are merged into one standardized profile.
- The system creates an instant, doctor-ready summary.
- If red-flag emergency symptoms are detected (for example chest tightness or stroke signs), triage nurses are alerted immediately.

### 5) Doctor Encounter
- Doctor opens a clean summary before seeing the patient.
- Consultation time is used for focused examination and treatment decisions rather than manual data gathering.

## Expected Outcome
MediKiosk reduces pre-consultation friction, improves capture of clinically relevant details, supports safer triage, and helps doctors deliver higher-quality care despite heavy OPD load.
